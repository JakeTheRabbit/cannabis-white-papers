Paper text grew from 204,000 to 284,000 words (+39%): STE needs more, shorter sentences. Site chrome (navigation, search, home page), the generated corpus texts (`llms.txt`, markdown separators) and hand-drawn SVG strings were also converted.

## Checks that pass
- `ste_verify.py check` on all 55 papers: PASS (0 errors in structure, numbers, citations, links and in the lint of the rendered page). Run all: `C:\Tools\ste-lint\.venv\Scripts\python.exe _build/ste100/ste_verify_all.py`.
- `check_section_structure.py` and `check_generated_structure.py`: OK for 55 papers (two source-note titles updated in the gate table).
- Built site compared with a build of the original commit (`ste_site_compare.py`): same sections, figures, tables, lists, callouts and citations on every page. Only the tissue-culture photo gallery moved (to the section "The terms and facts of tissue culture"). Auto cross-links: 456 before, 480 after.
- Every figure label was checked against its box (measured text widths and a browser render by the diagram agents).

## What the checks cannot prove
- The linter is an aid, not a certificate. It checks the 875-word dictionary, technical nouns and verbs, and the writing rules. It does not reliably catch an approved word used with the wrong meaning or part of speech (for example `come`, `put`, `by`, `light`, `level`). The agents checked these by hand with `ste_lookup.py`; a human STE reviewer should still sample the text.
- Technical nouns: 3,449 words were added to per-paper lists; 188 are flagged by `ste_tn_audit.py` as general English or as approved words used in other senses (examples: cost, market, price, line, page, document). They are the main residual risk.
- Fixed headings stay as named exceptions because the gates enforce them: Purpose and scope, Definitions, Evidence and limitations, Expected results and limitations, References.
- Where STE cannot say the original, the agents recast it and listed the loss as "Nuance lost / needs review" in each report (`paper_*.md`). Examples: analogies and idioms removed (the mechanism is stated instead), "approximately" for "~", numbers kept, vague counts such as "hundreds" made explicit.

## Residual scan of the built site
`ste_site_lint.py` lints the text of every built page (navigation, evidence panels, captions, glossary and index included) with the global lexicon. Before the last cleanup it reported 3,865 findings at the 45-paper stage and 123 at the end. The last real ones were fixed: eight section titles with an -ing form were recast ("Select and cut shoots", "Size of the system", "Time of harvest" ...), and 14 glossary term names were changed to American spelling or approved words ("Acclimatization (hardening)", "Grams per liter (g/L)", "Moving average (rolling average)", "Common-cause variation and special-cause variation"; twelve "X vs Y" names became "X and Y"). What remains is extractor noise: the page title is drawn twice (so the extractor glues it to the next text, for example "cuttingshow"), `&ndash;` entities read as semicolons, and the heuristic mode (no spaCy) reports a few false perfect or progressive tenses.
